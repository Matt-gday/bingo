# Image brief for the bingo game

You are creating all the artwork for a cute mobile bingo game (working title: Solo Bingo). This file tells you what to make, how it must look, and where to save it. Please read all of it before generating anything.

## 1. What the game is

A single-player bingo game for phones. The player competes against a few computer-controlled regulars at a friendly bingo club, shouts "BINGO!" into the phone's microphone to win, and spends the credits they earn at a prize table.

The prizes are things people enjoy collecting: craft supplies, stationery, soft toys, instruments, sports gear and treats, mixed with a few daggy things you might win at an Australian club night, like a meat tray or a tea towel. Each prize belongs to one or more sets, such as Stationery, Pink or Fluffy, and finishing a set earns a badge.

The game should appeal to a 12-year-old who loves drawing, cutting and making things, and to her parent. The mood is fun, cute, friendly and a little bit silly.

## 2. Look at the mockups first

Every dashed box labelled "Prize art", "Caller character art", "Logo art", "Set badge art" or "Bingo club scene art" is a place one of your images will go.

The round smiley faces used for the players are drawn by the app. Do not make any player faces.

## 3. Style rules for every image

- Cute 3D render in a toy-like style: soft rounded shapes, slightly chunky proportions, smooth glossy surfaces, gentle studio lighting.
- The light always comes from the upper left, with a soft highlight on the upper left of each object.
- Bright, saturated colours that sit well with the app's palette (listed below). Food and natural objects can keep their natural colours, but make them bright and clean, never dull or muddy.
- Simple and bold. Most images are shown very small on a phone, about the size of a thumbnail, so avoid fine detail and thin parts.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names or watermarks on anything, unless this brief asks for text.
- No realistic humans.
- Every image must look like part of one matching set: same style, lighting, camera angle and level of detail.

### What the tags mean for the artwork

Each prize in section 7B lists its tags. Most are for the game and need nothing from you, but eight of them must be visible in the picture:

- **Pink, Purple, Aqua:** the object must be clearly and mostly that colour.
- **Rainbow:** the object must show several bright colours together.
- **Fluffy:** it must look soft and fuzzy.
- **Sparkly:** it must have visible glitter, shine or small star sparkles.
- **Mini:** it must look tiny and dainty, for example in a little pack, on a keyring, or with stubby proportions.
- **Animals:** it must be an animal or clearly shaped like one.

## 4. Technical rules

- **Format:** PNG.
- **Transparency:** prizes, characters, badges, the gem and the logo need a truly transparent background. No backdrop, no floor, no ground shadow, and never a painted grey-and-white checkerboard.
- **Size:** 1024 x 1024 for everything square. The app will shrink them.
- **Framing:** one subject, centred, at a slight three-quarter angle. Keep the whole subject inside the middle 80 percent of the canvas so nothing touches the edges.
- **Scene and app icon:** these two have filled backgrounds. Their details are in section 7.

## 5. Folders and file names

Save everything inside the `Bingo App` folder using this structure. Create any folders that don't exist.

```
Bingo App/
  Mockups/                 (already exists, do not change)
  Images/
    prizes/                prize-<name>.png
    characters/            caller-<expression>.png
    badges/                badge-<set>.png
    scenes/                scene-club.png
    logo/                  logo.png, app-icon.png
    icons/                 gem.png
    _drafts/               anything not yet approved
    image-list.md          your running list of what is done
```

Naming rules:

- Lowercase letters only, with hyphens between words. No spaces, capitals or underscores.
- Use the exact file names given in the tables below.
- Do not add version numbers. If an image is replaced, move the old one into `_drafts` and save the new one under the same name.
- Keep `Images/image-list.md` up to date with one line per image: the file name and either "done" or "needs redo".

## 6. How to work

1. **Make the five test images first** (section 7A), save them, show them to me, and stop. Do not continue until I say they are approved.
2. Once approved, treat `prize-pink-pencil.png` as the style anchor. Every later image must match it.
3. Work through the rest in this order: prizes, caller expressions, badges, gem, scene, app icon. Leave the logo until I confirm the game's name.
4. Work in batches of about six. After each batch, show me the images and check each one against the checklist in section 8.
5. If an image fails the checklist, redo it before moving on.

## 8. Checklist for every image

Before saving an image as done, check that:

- The background is truly transparent (except the scene and app icon).
- The subject is centred with clear space on all sides.
- There is no text, number, logo or watermark (except the logo itself).
- It matches the style, lighting and angle of `prize-pink-pencil.png`.
- It still reads clearly when shrunk to about 100 pixels wide.
- Any colour or feel tag (Pink, Purple, Aqua, Rainbow, Fluffy, Sparkly, Mini, Animals) is obvious in the picture.
- The file name and folder are exactly as listed.
