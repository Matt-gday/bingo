Speech bubble mock-up

The bubble is a fixed size, so nothing around it ever moves. Longer lines scroll up inside it. Lines leaving at the top fade and blur away, and the next line peeks in, faint and blurred, at the bottom. The scrolling follows how long the caller takes to say the line.

  • Game header: the bubble beside the number ball is the narrowest place, about 138 pixels wide. Two versions are shown, the type as it is now and a smaller one. Three lines fit; the peek of a fourth shows only when the line is longer.
  • Welcome: the bigger caller before the first number. Four lines, smaller type, beside the "?" ball.
  • Check screen: the large caller and bubble. Three lines at a larger size.
  • Short lines like "Legs eleven!" sit still in the middle of the bubble, with no scrolling.
  • "Speed" is how many characters the caller says each second. Recorded lines run at about 14.
