Four quick rules before we start.

1

Two cards, one mark

Each call, mark one square on either card. Nothing is marked for you.

2

Beat the ring

Move your mark while the ring runs. When it closes, the mark locks for good.

3

Shout it

Think you have a line? Tap Call bingo, then shout BINGO out loud.

4

Be sure

I check every number. A wrong card means you sit out two calls.
